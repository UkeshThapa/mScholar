# Project: **MouseScholar**

The mouseScholar is the extension project used for the researcher to search the research paper and used to find the top related research paper. It won't stop there it will help to find what concept or paper you should read first. 

## Features
1. Highlighted title or text will be search to find the research paper
2. Displays number of citation where it is published cite type bitex, APA, MLA save in the zotero
3. Top five related papers
4. Paper you have to read before that concept

## Frontend features:

When we shake the mouse the extension will be activated and you use it for the search

Hightlight and right click will help you to open a preview with important information on title of research paper.
